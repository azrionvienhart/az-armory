[ II ] — How it works
01 / Profile

Tell it how you train.

Pick Aimlabs, KovaaK's or Mobile, then your aim level. The platform decides which real scenarios go into your routine.

02 / Confession

Describe what goes wrong.

Type it in plain words, like "I overflick when they dash". Azware names the problem, gives advice and shifts your focus toward the skill you need.

Keyword matching, no AI, works offline.

03 / Quests

Get today's 12 quests.

Split across flicking, switching and tracking in your exact ratio, in the same order as Aimlabs' official routine. A new set rotates in every day.

04 / Sensitivity

Find your sensitivity.

Seven rounds of "lower or higher?". It uses the same algorithm as the popular online PSA calculators, and the result is your Rivals Sensitivity %.

[ IV ] — Straight talk

What it is. What it isn't.

It is

  • An aim trainer / planner. It builds your routine; you play it in Aimlabs, KovaaK's or the built-in trainer.
  • Built on real scenarios, including Aimlabs' official Roblox Rivals Aim Basics Routine.
  • Private. No account and no tracking. Progress lives in your own browser.
  • Free. No ads, no premium tier.

It isn't

  • A cheat or aim assist. It never touches Roblox.
  • An AI chatbot. The issues box is a keyword matcher.
  • Connected to Aimlabs or KovaaK's. It lists scenario names, and you play them yourself.
  • Affiliated with Roblox, the Rivals developers, Aimlabs or KovaaK's.
